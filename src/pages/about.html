About

Mathematics, research, and applied AI—connected by problem solving.

I am Zahra Taheri, an applied machine-learning engineer and researcher. My work brings rigorous modeling into practical AI systems and keeps evaluation close to implementation.

I began with a PhD in Mathematics, studying abstract structures and rigorous reasoning. I then moved into graph machine learning through independent study and hands-on experiments, building research experience in graph representation learning, molecular property prediction, knowledge graphs, and healthcare-oriented machine learning.

In industry, I applied that analytical foundation to a computer-vision product: translating clinical and product requirements into a multi-view AI workflow, testing model behavior, building validation and quality-control components, and integrating outputs into the application.

More recently, my independent LLM Systems Lab work has focused on the engineering foundations behind retrieval-based AI: inspectable evidence, deterministic evaluation, failure analysis, and maintainable components.

Across these settings, I aim to be precise about what a system does, how it was evaluated, and where its limitations remain.
